Instant coffee protein shake

By Koby Stull · October 29, 2026

Dissolve one serving of instant coffee in 2 fl oz of hot water, then blend it with 6 fl oz of cold milk, one scoop of vanilla or chocolate protein powder and a handful of ice for 20 seconds. No blender? Shake the cooled coffee base, milk and powder hard in a shaker bottle.

  • Prep 3 min
  • Total 3 min
  • Makes 1 shake (about 12 fl oz)

Ingredients

  • 1 serving instant coffee (one level scoop of ours, or the serving size on your jar)
  • 2 fl oz hot water
  • 6 fl oz cold milk (any kind)
  • 1 scoop protein powder (vanilla or chocolate; use the scoop in your tub)
  • A handful of ice

Steps

  1. Dissolve the instant coffee in the hot water.
  2. Add the cold milk to the blender first, then the protein powder, then the coffee base and the ice. Liquid first stops powder sticking to the bottom.
  3. Blend for about 20 seconds until smooth.
  4. Pour and drink. If it's too thick, add a splash of milk and blend for two seconds.

Tips

  • In a shaker bottle, let the coffee base cool for a minute first; hot liquid builds pressure in a sealed bottle.
  • Unflavored powder tastes better with a teaspoon of honey or a few drops of vanilla.
  • Read the protein powder's label too: some contain caffeine or other active ingredients of their own.

Questions

Can I put protein powder straight into hot coffee?

Many protein powders clump or turn stringy in very hot liquid. Blend or shake them with cold milk and add the coffee base to that.

Does this work with plant-based protein?

Yes. Plant proteins are often thicker, so add an extra splash of milk.
