Instant coffee subscription: what to check before you sign up

By Koby Stull · Updated October 30, 2026

An instant coffee subscription ships a bag or jar on a set schedule, usually for less than buying it once. Before you sign up, check four things: the cost per cup including shipping, whether the delivery interval matches how fast you drink it, how to pause or cancel online, and what happens if a bag arrives damaged.

How they work

You pick a product and an interval, such as every 30 days. The store charges your card and ships a new bag each time, at a lower price than a one-time order, until you pause or cancel. The details that matter are in the terms, not the headline discount.

1. Cost per cup, with shipping

Divide what you pay per delivery, shipping included, by the servings in it. A subscription price that looks cheaper can cost more per cup if shipping is charged on every delivery and the one-time order ships free, so compare both on the same basis. The cost-per-cup calculator does the math.

2. Match the interval to how much you drink

Servings in a bag ÷ cups a day = days the bag lasts. If that's shorter than the interval, you'll run out before the next one arrives; if it's longer, bags pile up.

If your use changes, look for a way to change the interval or skip a delivery rather than cancelling.

3. Pausing and cancelling

  • Where: can you pause, skip or cancel online, or do you have to call or email?
  • When: what's the cut-off before the next charge?
  • Minimums: is there a minimum number of deliveries or a cancellation fee?
  • Reminders: do you get an email before each charge?

4. Damaged bags and returns

Find the window for reporting a damaged or wrong delivery, whether opened bags can be returned, and who pays return shipping. Food products often can't be returned once opened.

Questions

Is an instant coffee subscription cheaper than buying once?

Usually per bag. Compare the cost per cup with shipping included, since some stores charge shipping on every delivery. Ours is $10 less per bag than a one-time order.

Can I pause an instant coffee subscription?

Many stores allow it; check the terms before you sign up. Ours can be paused, skipped or cancelled from the link in any receipt.

What if I drink more than one cup a day?

Divide the servings in a bag by your cups a day. At two cups a day, a 30-serving bag lasts 15 days, so a 30-day interval would leave you short.

Do I have to call to cancel?

It depends on the store. Ours is cancelled online from the receipt link or the subscription portal, with no call and no fee.
