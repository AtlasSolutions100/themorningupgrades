About

Two people, one bag of coffee, and a website that says what it is.

Rector & Stull LLC is a small company run by two partners, one in Idaho and one in Texas. We're independent distributors for MarketPlace Global, and CoreJava is the product we drink ourselves, which is why it's the one we sell.

We started The Morning Upgrade because most coffee marketing is either a lifestyle fantasy or a wall of supplement jargon. We wanted a page that tells you what's in the bag, what it costs per cup, and how to find out in a week whether it's for you.

Everything on this site that earns us money says so. Every video that uses an AI-generated scene is labeled. If something's unclear, email us and a human will answer: hello@themorningupgrades.com.
