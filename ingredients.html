The seven ingredients

MCT powder

First on the label

Medium-chain triglycerides, usually from coconut oil, in powder form. It’s what gives the cup body and a creamy texture without adding cream.

Natural caffeine

From coffee beans

The same caffeine you already drink, from coffee, not synthesized. About the amount in a strong cup.

L-theanine

The amino acid in green tea

Found naturally in tea leaves. Commonly paired with caffeine; many people describe the pairing as smoother than coffee alone.

ChromeMate®

A patented chromium

A branded form of the mineral chromium, bound to niacin. Chromium is an essential trace mineral.

Paradoxine®

Grains of paradise extract

A standardized extract of Aframomum melegueta, a West African seed in the ginger family.

Garcinia Cambogia

Fruit extract

Extract of the rind of a small tropical fruit, standardized for hydroxycitric acid.

Saffr'Activ®

Saffron extract

A standardized extract of saffron stigmas, the same spice used in cooking.
