What's in it

What’s in CoreJava. Nothing hidden behind a blend.

We'll tell you where each one comes from. We describe the cup by what's in it: natural caffeine for energy, L-theanine alongside it, MCT powder for a creamy texture. We don't make health claims beyond the label.

The ingredients we talk about

MCT powder

First on the label

Medium-chain triglycerides, usually from coconut oil, in powder form. It’s what gives the cup body and a creamy texture without adding cream. More on MCT coffee.

Natural caffeine

From coffee beans

The same caffeine you already drink, from coffee, not synthesized. Each scoop has 100 mg of added natural caffeine, plus the instant coffee’s own.

L-theanine

The amino acid in green tea

Found naturally in tea leaves. Commonly paired with caffeine; many people describe the pairing as smoother than coffee alone. What the research says.

ChromeMate®

A patented chromium

A branded form of the mineral chromium, bound to niacin. Chromium is an essential trace mineral.

Paradoxine®

Grains of paradise extract

A standardized extract of Aframomum melegueta, a West African seed in the ginger family.

Garcinia Cambogia

Fruit extract

Extract of the rind of a small tropical fruit, standardized for hydroxycitric acid.

Saffr'Activ®

Saffron extract

A standardized extract of saffron stigmas, the same spice used in cooking.
