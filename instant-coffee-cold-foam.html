Instant coffee cold foam

By Koby Stull · October 29, 2026

Froth 3 fl oz of cold skim or 2% milk with a teaspoon of sugar using a handheld frother for about 20 seconds until it's thick and glossy, then spoon it over a glass of iced instant coffee. Skim milk makes the stiffest cold foam; whole milk makes a softer, richer one.

  • Prep 3 min
  • Total 3 min
  • Makes 1 glass

Ingredients

  • 1 serving instant coffee (one level scoop of ours, or the serving size on your jar)
  • 2 fl oz hot water
  • 1 glass of ice and 4 fl oz cold water
  • 3 fl oz cold skim or 2% milk
  • 1 tsp sugar or 1 tsp vanilla syrup

Steps

  1. Make an iced coffee: dissolve the instant coffee in the hot water, pour it over ice, and top with the cold water.
  2. Put the cold milk and sugar in a tall glass or jar.
  3. Froth with a handheld frother, held just below the surface, for about 20 seconds until thick.
  4. Spoon the foam over the iced coffee. It floats for several minutes.

Tips

  • The milk must be fridge-cold; warm milk won't hold a cold foam.
  • No frother? Shake the milk and sugar hard in a lidded jar for a minute.
  • Plant milks foam less; barista-style versions work best.

Questions

Why skim milk for cold foam?

Milk proteins build the foam and fat weighs it down, so lower-fat milk whips stiffer when cold.

Can I put coffee in the foam?

Yes: dissolve a pinch of instant coffee in a teaspoon of hot water, let it cool, and froth it with the milk. Count it as part of your coffee.
