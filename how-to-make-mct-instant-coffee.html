How to make instant MCT coffee (one scoop, 30 seconds)

By Koby Stull · Updated October 28, 2026

Put one level scoop (about 6.8 g) in a mug, pour in 8 fl oz of hot water, and stir until no grains are left, about 15 seconds. That's one serving. Drink it black, add creamer, or dissolve it in a little hot water and pour it over ice.

Step by step

  1. Scoop. One level scoop, leveled off against the edge of the bag. That's about 6.8 g, the serving size on the label.
  2. Add hot water. 8 fl oz (one standard mug) from a kettle or hot-water tap. Hot water dissolves the powder completely; lukewarm water leaves grains.
  3. Stir. About 15 seconds, until the surface is smooth.
  4. Adjust. Add creamer, milk or sweetener if you like. It's a medium roast and works black.

Getting the strength right

Change the water, not the scoop. A little less water makes a bolder cup and a little more makes it milder. One scoop is the serving: a second scoop doubles the caffeine as well as the flavor.

Habits that help

  • Have it in the morning. It contains caffeine.
  • One scoop a day means a 30-serving bag is a month of mornings.
  • Close the bag after each scoop and keep it somewhere dry; moisture makes any powder clump.

Questions

Can I make it with cold water?

Not directly; powders dissolve slowly in cold water and clump. Stir the scoop into about 2 oz of hot water first, then add ice and cold water.

Can I use milk instead of water?

Yes. Heat the milk first, or dissolve the scoop in a splash of hot water and then add the milk, hot or cold.

How much caffeine is in a scoop?

About the same as a strong cup of coffee. The exact amount is on the Supplement Facts panel on the bag.

Do I need a machine or a blender?

No. A mug, hot water and a spoon. The MCT is already in powder form, so nothing needs blending.

How long does a bag last?

A bag has 30 servings, so one scoop a day lasts 30 days.
