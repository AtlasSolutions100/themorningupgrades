How to make instant MCT coffee (1 scoop, 30 seconds)

By Koby Stull · Updated October 30, 2026

Put 1 level scoop (about 6.8 g) in a mug, pour in 8 fl oz of hot water, and stir until no grains are left, about 15 seconds. That's 1 serving. Drink it black, add creamer, or dissolve it in a little hot water and pour it over ice.

Step by step

  1. Scoop. 1 level scoop, leveled off against the edge of the bag. That's about 6.8 g, the serving size on the label.
  2. Add hot water. 8 fl oz (1 standard mug) from a kettle or hot-water tap. Hot water dissolves the powder completely; lukewarm water leaves grains.
  3. Stir. About 15 seconds, until the surface is smooth.
  4. Adjust. Add creamer, milk or sweetener if you like. It's a dark roast and works black.

Getting the strength right

Change the water, not the scoop. A little less water makes a bolder cup and a little more makes it milder. 1 scoop is the serving: a second scoop doubles the caffeine as well as the flavor.

Instant coffee with MCT oil instead

If you have plain instant coffee and a bottle of MCT oil, you can make the same kind of cup; it just needs mixing.

  1. Make the coffee. Dissolve your usual serving of instant coffee in 8 fl oz of hot water.
  2. Add the oil. Use the serving on the MCT oil bottle; many labels suggest starting with a smaller amount.
  3. Froth or blend. A handheld frother for 15–20 seconds, or a blender. Stirred oil just floats on top.

The powder version skips step 3: MCT powder is MCT oil dried onto a carrier so it disperses with a spoon. That’s why pre-mixed instant MCT coffees use powder.

Habits that help

  • Have it in the morning. It contains caffeine.
  • 1 scoop a day means a 30-serving bag is a month of mornings.
  • Close the bag after each scoop and keep it somewhere dry; moisture makes any powder clump.

Questions

Can I make it with cold water?

Not directly; powders dissolve slowly in cold water and clump. Stir the scoop into about 2 oz of hot water first, then add ice and cold water.

Can I use milk instead of water?

Yes. Heat the milk first, or dissolve the scoop in a splash of hot water and then add the milk, hot or cold.

How much caffeine is in a scoop?

100 mg of added natural caffeine, plus the instant coffee's own. See the Supplement Facts panel on the bag.

Do I need a machine or a blender?

No. A mug, hot water and a spoon. The MCT is already in powder form, so nothing needs blending.

Can I add MCT oil to instant coffee?

Yes. Dissolve the instant coffee in hot water, add the MCT oil at the serving on its bottle, and froth or blend it; stirred oil floats on top. MCT powder stirs in without blending.

How long does a bag last?

A bag has 30 servings, so 1 scoop a day lasts 30 days.
