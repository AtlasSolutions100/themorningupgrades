Instant coffee latte

By Koby Stull · October 29, 2026

Dissolve one serving of instant coffee in 2 fl oz of hot water to make a strong base, then top it with 6 fl oz of hot milk you've frothed until it's foamy. A jar, a whisk or a handheld frother all work; you don't need an espresso machine.

  • Prep 2 min
  • Total 4 min
  • Makes 1 mug (about 8 fl oz)

Ingredients

  • 1 serving instant coffee (one level scoop of ours, or the serving size on your jar)
  • 2 fl oz hot water
  • 6 fl oz milk (whole milk foams most easily; oat and soy work too)
  • Sweetener to taste (optional)

Steps

  1. Heat the milk until it steams and small bubbles form at the edge: about 60 to 90 seconds in the microwave, or a few minutes in a small pan. Don't let it boil.
  2. Froth the hot milk: use a handheld frother for 20 seconds, whisk it hard in the pan, or shake it in a lidded jar half full.
  3. Dissolve the instant coffee in the hot water in your mug.
  4. Pour the milk in, holding back the foam with a spoon, then spoon the foam on top.

Tips

  • Milk tastes sweetest at drinking-hot, not boiling; scorched milk tastes flat.
  • For a flat white, use less milk (4 fl oz) and only a thin layer of foam.
  • Shaking hot milk in a jar works, but leave room in the jar and hold the lid with a towel.

Questions

Can I make a latte without a frother?

Yes. Shake the hot milk in a lidded jar for 30 seconds or whisk it hard in the pan. The foam is a little bigger-bubbled than a steam wand's, but it works.

What's the difference between a latte and a cappuccino?

Mostly the milk: a latte has more milk and a thin foam layer; a cappuccino has less milk and a thicker cap of foam.
