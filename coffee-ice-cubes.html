Coffee ice cubes

By Koby Stull · October 29, 2026

Dissolve instant coffee in hot water at normal drinking strength, let it cool, pour it into an ice-cube tray and freeze it for about four hours. Use the cubes in iced coffee so melting ice adds coffee instead of water.

  • Prep 5 min
  • Total 4 hr 5 min
  • Makes about 12 cubes (one 8 fl oz cup of coffee)

Ingredients

  • 1 serving instant coffee (one level scoop of ours, or the serving size on your jar)
  • 8 fl oz hot water
  • An ice-cube tray

Steps

  1. Make a normal cup: dissolve the instant coffee in the hot water.
  2. Let it cool to room temperature, about 20 minutes, so it doesn't warp the tray or warm the freezer.
  3. Pour it into the tray and freeze for about four hours, until solid.
  4. Pop the cubes into a freezer bag. Use them within a couple of weeks; they pick up freezer smells after that.

Tips

  • Freeze milk-coffee cubes for an iced latte that gets creamier as it melts.
  • Put three cubes in a glass of cold milk for a slow-melting iced latte.
  • Every cube is coffee, so the caffeine counts: a tray made from one cup is one cup's worth.
  • Silicone trays release frozen coffee more easily than rigid plastic ones; twist, then press from below.
  • Label the bag with the date so you use the oldest cubes first.

Questions

Do coffee ice cubes need to be strong coffee?

Normal strength works: as they melt they add coffee, so the drink holds its strength instead of thinning out.

Can I make them from leftover brewed coffee?

Yes. Any coffee you'd drink works; leftover morning coffee is the classic use.
