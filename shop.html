What’s in the scoop.

Four things you will taste or notice, with the amounts printed on the bag.

  • 3,000mg

    Dark roast Colombian instant coffee

    The coffee itself: three grams of dark roast in every scoop, a little smoother than drip. Good black, with milk, or over ice.

  • 400mg

    L-theanine

    An amino acid found in tea leaves, where it occurs alongside tea’s own caffeine. Here it is paired with coffee. What the pairing is.

  • 100mg

    Natural caffeine Coffea robusta seed

    Added on top of the instant coffee’s own caffeine, so count both toward your day.

  • From coconut

    MCT powder MCT oil and acacia gum

    Medium-chain triglycerides from coconut, already a powder. It gives the cup a rounded, creamy body without cream, and it stirs in with a spoon. What MCT coffee is.
