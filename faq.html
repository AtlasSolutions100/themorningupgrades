The coffee

Does it taste like coffee?

Yes. Dark roast, a little smoother than drip. Black is fine; creamer is fine; over ice is fine.

How much caffeine is in a serving?

Each scoop has 100 mg of added natural caffeine, plus the instant coffee’s own. See the Supplement Facts panel on the bag. It contains caffeine, so skip it late in the day if you're sensitive.

Is it 1 cup or 2?

1 scoop is the serving on the label.

What does it do?

It's instant coffee with natural caffeine, L-theanine and MCT powder, made for focus and steady energy. It's a dietary supplement, not a medicine, and it hasn't been evaluated by the FDA.

Who shouldn't take it?

Children, and anyone pregnant or nursing. If you take medication or have a medical condition, ask your physician first.
