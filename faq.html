FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

The coffee

Does it taste like coffee?

Yes. Medium roast, a little smoother than drip. Black is fine; creamer is fine; over ice is fine.

How much caffeine is in a serving?

About the same as a strong cup of coffee. The exact amount is on the Supplement Facts panel on the bag. It contains caffeine, so skip it late in the day if you're sensitive.

Is it one cup or two?

One scoop is the serving. Most people find one is the right amount.

What does it do?

The label says: supports healthy appetite control, focus and energy. We stick to that. It's a dietary supplement, not a medicine, and it hasn't been evaluated by the FDA.

Who shouldn't take it?

Children, and anyone pregnant or nursing. If you take medication or have a medical condition, ask your physician first.

Ordering

How does Subscribe & Save work?

A bag ships every 30 days at $69.95. Pause a month, cancel, or update your card any time from the link in your receipt or at the subscription portal. No calls.

Right now orders are fulfilled by the maker, MarketPlace Global, and shipping is calculated at their checkout before you pay. US addresses only for now. Tracking arrives by email.

Returns?

If a bag arrives damaged or wrong, email hello@themorningupgrades.com within 14 days and we'll replace it or refund it. Full policy on the Shipping & returns page.

Who am I buying from?

Rector & Stull LLC, Independent ISR of MarketPlace Global products and services. MarketPlace Global makes CoreJava; we sell it.

The business

Is this an MLM?

MarketPlace Global is a direct sales company that sells through Independent Sales Representatives (ISRs), and ISRs can sponsor other ISRs. We earn on the coffee we sell and on sales made by partners we introduce. If that interests you, the Partner page explains it without the hype.

Who are the people in your videos?
