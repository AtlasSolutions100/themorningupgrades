How it works

Enroll with MarketPlace Global

You sign up as an Independent Sales Representative under our team. MarketPlace Global handles product, shipping, payments and commissions.

Learn how we do it

MarketPlace Global provides the training, tools and approved materials. We add a weekly call where we walk through what we post, what we say, and the compliance rules that keep it clean.

Sell, then teach

You earn on the coffee you sell. If you introduce partners, you earn on what they sell too. We meet weekly to go over what's working.

Who this is for

  • You drink coffee every day and you'd rather sell one thing you use than ten you don't.
  • You're comfortable posting short videos, or willing to learn with templates that do most of the work.
  • You're fine with honest numbers. We'll show you ours.

Who it isn't for

Anyone who needs income this month. Anyone who wants to recruit before they've sold a bag. Anyone who'd rather not identify themselves as an independent MarketPlace Global ISR in everything they post.

The honest part

It costs money to start

MarketPlace Global charges an enrollment fee and you'll want product to try. We'll tell you the current numbers before you sign anything.

Most people earn little

That's true of every direct-sales company, and the income disclosure statement shows it. We'd rather you read it first than hear it later.

Results take work

Our system shortens the learning curve. It does not remove the work: posting, replying, following up, every week.

Rector & Stull LLC does not guarantee any level of income. MarketPlace Global’s own income disclosure projects average annual gross revenue for ISRs of roughly $500 to $2,000 before expenses, which can exceed commissions.
