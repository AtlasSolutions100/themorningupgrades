Instant coffee frappé

By Koby Stull · October 29, 2026

Dissolve 1 serving of instant coffee in 2 fl oz of hot water, then blend it with 4 fl oz of cold milk, a cup of ice and a teaspoon of honey until smooth and thick, about 30 seconds. Drink it right away; blended ice drinks start separating within minutes.

  • Prep 3 min
  • Total 3 min
  • Makes 1 glass (about 12 fl oz)

Ingredients

  • 1 serving instant coffee (1 level scoop of ours, or the serving size on your jar)
  • 2 fl oz hot water
  • 4 fl oz cold milk (any kind)
  • 1 cup ice
  • 1 tsp honey or sugar

Steps

  1. Dissolve the instant coffee and the honey in the hot water.
  2. Add the coffee base, cold milk and ice to a blender.
  3. Blend on high for 20 to 30 seconds, until the ice is fully crushed and the drink is thick.
  4. Pour into a chilled glass and drink straight away.

Tips

  • For a thicker frappé, freeze the milk in an ice tray the night before and use those cubes instead of plain ice.
  • Half a frozen banana makes it thick and sweet without extra sugar.
  • If your blender struggles, add the liquid first and the ice last.
  • For a mocha frappé, add a teaspoon of cocoa powder to the hot coffee base before blending.

Questions

Why does my frappé separate?

Blended ice and liquid separate as the ice melts. Frozen milk cubes or a little banana keep it together longer.

Is a frappé the same as a frappuccino?

Frappuccino is a coffee-chain trademark for its blended drinks. A frappé is the general name for a blended or shaken iced coffee.
