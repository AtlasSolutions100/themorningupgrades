What’s in it

Seven ingredients have amounts on the Supplement Facts panel: the dark roast Colombian instant coffee itself, L-theanine (the amino acid found in tea leaves), natural caffeine, ChromeMate® chromium, Garcinia cambogia, Paradoxine® grains of paradise extract and Saffr’Activ® saffron extract. The MCT powder, made from coconut MCT oil and acacia gum, is what makes the cup creamy without cream. Each ingredient, explained.

How to make it

  1. Hot: one level scoop in a mug, 8 fl oz of hot water, stir about 15 seconds.
  2. Iced: dissolve the scoop in 2 fl oz of hot water, pour over a full glass of ice, top with 6 fl oz of cold water or milk.
  3. Latte-style: dissolve the scoop in 2 fl oz of hot water, then add 6 fl oz of steamed or frothed milk.

No machine and no blender: the MCT is already a powder. One scoop is the serving, so change the water, not the scoop, to adjust the strength. Step-by-step guide · 12 recipes.
