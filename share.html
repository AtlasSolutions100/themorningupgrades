In plain language

What “Share” actually is

  • It’s MarketPlace Global’s Independent Sales Representative program. MPG is the direct-sales company that makes CoreJava. We’re ISRs too; if you join through us, we’re your sponsor and the people on the weekly call.
  • It costs money to start. The CoreJava Starter Kit is $179.90 (two bags plus two 6-day experience packs) and the Builder Kit is $229.90 (three of each). Both include the $29.95 annual membership. Plus shipping and tax.
  • You earn on product sales, not on signing people up. A Retail Customer Bonus every time someone you referred orders, and a Fast Start Bonus if someone you introduce enrolls with a kit. MPG pays weekly.
  • To stay eligible for team commissions you keep 50 QV a month, which is roughly one bag of your own or one customer order. In the Share level you can do this by drinking the coffee you were already buying.
  • Most participants earn little. MPG’s own disclosure projects average annual gross revenue of roughly $500 to $2,000 before expenses, which can exceed what you earn. Read it: income disclosure statement. Also read the Policies & Procedures and Compensation Plan.
  • You can stop whenever. Don’t renew the membership and it lapses. 30-day money-back on unopened product bought as an ISR.

If that all sounds fine and you mostly want your coffee covered, Share is for you. If you read it and thought “I could do more than three friends,” look at Build.

Questions people ask before they share

Do I have to recruit anyone?

No. The Share level is customers only: friends who buy coffee. Nobody is paid for recruiting at any level; compensation is on product sales.

Is this an MLM?

MarketPlace Global is a direct-sales company whose compensation plan includes sponsoring other reps, so yes, it’s in that category. We’d rather you know that on this page than find out later.

What do my friends pay?

The same prices as anyone else: $79.95 a bag or $69.95 a month on Subscribe & Save, plus shipping. Your link doesn’t change their price.

What if I already have an MPG account?

Then you already have a sponsor and MPG policy doesn’t allow switching. Keep sharing through your own link; we’re happy to compare notes.

Do I need to buy inventory?

No. Friends order from MPG directly through your link and MPG ships to them. The kit is the only purchase.
