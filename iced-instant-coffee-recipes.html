How to make iced instant coffee without clumps (3 recipes)

By Koby Stull · Updated October 29, 2026

Instant coffee and ice water don't mix. One small trick fixes it, then three recipes worth keeping.

The trick: dissolve hot, then go cold

Powders dissolve slowly in cold water and clump. Stir your scoop into about 2 oz of hot water first until it's fully dissolved, then pour it over ice and top up with cold water or milk. Thirty seconds, no lumps.

1. Iced classic

  • 1 serving instant coffee, dissolved in 2 oz hot water
  • A full glass of ice
  • 6 oz cold water

Iced coffee tastes stronger than hot, so add water to taste.

2. Iced oat vanilla

  • 1 serving dissolved in 2 oz hot water
  • Ice and 6 oz oat milk
  • 1 drop vanilla extract

One drop of vanilla. Two is a candle.

3. Blended frappé

  • 1 serving dissolved in 2 oz hot water
  • 4 oz milk, 1 cup ice, 1 tsp honey

Blend until smooth and drink it before it separates.
