Instant coffee affogato

By Koby Stull · October 29, 2026

Dissolve one serving of instant coffee in just 2 fl oz of very hot water to make a short, strong shot, then pour it over a scoop of vanilla ice cream in a small glass and eat it straight away with a spoon.

  • Prep 2 min
  • Total 2 min
  • Makes 1 small dessert

Ingredients

  • 1 serving instant coffee (one level scoop of ours, or the serving size on your jar)
  • 2 fl oz very hot water
  • 1 scoop vanilla ice cream (or gelato)
  • Shaved dark chocolate (optional)

Steps

  1. Put a scoop of ice cream in a small glass or cup. Chill the glass first if you have time.
  2. Dissolve the instant coffee in the very hot water. Keep it short; the small volume is what makes it taste like espresso.
  3. Pour the hot coffee over the ice cream at the table.
  4. Add a little shaved chocolate if you like and eat it while it's half melted.

Tips

  • Gelato is denser than ice cream and melts slower, so it holds up to the hot coffee better.
  • Try it with chocolate or salted caramel ice cream.
  • It's a dessert with a full serving of coffee in it, so it carries the same caffeine as your morning cup.
  • Serve it in a small, wide glass so the coffee pools around the scoop instead of sinking straight under it.
  • For a crunchy finish, add a crumbled amaretti or shortbread cookie on top.

Questions

What does affogato mean?

It's Italian for 'drowned': ice cream drowned in coffee. It's traditionally made with espresso.

Can I make it dairy-free?

Yes. Use a dairy-free vanilla ice cream; coconut- and oat-based ones both work.
