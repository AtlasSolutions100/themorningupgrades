How much caffeine is in instant coffee?

By Koby Stull · Updated October 30, 2026

USDA data puts a cup of instant coffee made with water at about 26 mg of caffeine per 100 g, roughly 62 mg in an 8 fl oz cup, versus about 95 mg brewed. The real number depends mostly on how much powder you use, so the label's serving is the figure that counts.

The numbers, side by side

Drink (USDA FoodData Central)Caffeine per 100 gAbout per 8 fl oz cup
Instant coffee, prepared with water26 mg~62 mg
Brewed coffee, prepared with tap water40 mg~95 mg
Instant coffee powder, dry3,142 mg~31 mg per level teaspoon (1 g)

The per-cup column multiplies USDA's per-100 g values by the mass of an 8 fl oz cup (about 237 g). USDA's own serving for prepared instant coffee is 6 fl oz, which works out to roughly 47 mg.

Why instant usually has less

Instant coffee is brewed coffee that has been dried to a powder. A cup made from it contains whatever caffeine is in the amount of powder you stir in. The usual one-teaspoon cup uses less coffee than a typical drip cup, which is why the average comes out lower. Use two teaspoons and you're in brewed-coffee territory.

What changes the number

  • How much powder. The single biggest factor. Caffeine scales with the scoop, not the water.
  • The beans. Robusta beans carry more caffeine than arabica, and many instant coffees use some robusta.
  • Added caffeine. Some instant coffees and blends add caffeine or caffeine-containing ingredients. Then the label, not the averages above, is the only reliable figure.
  • Decaf. Decaffeinated instant still contains a few milligrams per cup.

How it fits a day

For most healthy adults, the FDA cites 400 mg a day as an amount not generally associated with negative effects, and lists a 12 fl oz cup of regular brewed coffee at roughly 113–247 mg. Sensitivity varies, and pregnancy, medication and some conditions change the picture; ask your physician if that's you.

Questions

Does instant coffee have less caffeine than brewed?

Usually, per cup, because a typical instant cup uses less coffee. USDA's averages are about 62 mg for an 8 fl oz instant cup and about 95 mg for brewed. More powder means more caffeine.

How much caffeine is in a teaspoon of instant coffee?

USDA lists dry instant coffee powder at 3,142 mg per 100 g and a teaspoon at 1 g, so roughly 31 mg per level teaspoon. Heaped teaspoons hold more.

Is decaf instant coffee caffeine-free?

No. Decaffeination removes most caffeine but not all, so a cup still contains a small amount.

Does adding milk reduce the caffeine?

No. Milk dilutes the taste, not the amount of caffeine in the cup.

How do I know the caffeine in a functional instant coffee?

Read the Supplement Facts or Nutrition Facts panel. Products with added caffeine list it; averages for plain instant coffee don't apply to them.
