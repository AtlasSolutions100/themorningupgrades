Shipping & returns

Shipping

We ship to addresses in the United States. Orders are fulfilled by the maker, MarketPlace Global, from Maple Grove, Minnesota, usually within two business days, then 3–6 days in transit. You'll get a tracking email when it leaves.

Subscribe & Save

A new bag ships every 30 days and your card is charged $69.95 each time. You can pause a month or cancel from the link in any receipt, or at the subscription portal, up to the day before your next shipment. There are no cancellation fees.

Returns and refunds

MarketPlace Global, the maker, backs every customer order with a 90-day money-back guarantee on unopened product in resalable condition, and replaces anything that arrives damaged, defective or incorrect if you tell us within 30 calendar days of delivery. Email hello@themorningupgrades.com with your order number (and a photo if it’s damaged) and we handle the return authorization for you. Return shipping on change-of-mind returns is paid by the customer; original shipping isn’t refunded. Opened bags can’t be returned for a change of mind, for food-safety reasons.

Questions
