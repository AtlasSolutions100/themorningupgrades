Shipping & returns

Shipping

We ship to addresses in the United States. Ships from the maker directly. Orders placed by 2 PM Central on a business day ship that day or the next. Standard shipping is included in the price. You'll get a tracking email when it leaves.

Subscribe & Save

A new bag ships every 30 days and your card is charged $69.95 each time. You can pause, skip a month, or cancel from the link in any receipt, up to the day before your next shipment. There are no cancellation fees.

Returns and refunds

If your order arrives damaged, opened, or wrong, email hello@themorningupgrades.com within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photo, and we'll replace it or refund it in full. For unopened bags you simply don't want, email us within 30 days of delivery and we'll refund the price once the bag is back with us; return shipping is on you. Opened bags can't be returned for a change of mind, for food-safety reasons.
