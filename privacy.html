Policies

Privacy

This is the privacy policy for themorningupgrades.com, operated by Rector & Stull LLC ("we"). It's written to be read.

What we collect

  • If you take the quiz: your answers, first name and email, so we can send you the guide and the follow-up emails.
  • If you buy: your name, shipping address, email and phone (for the carrier). Payment details go to Stripe; we never see your card number.
  • If you ask about partnering: what you type into the form.
  • Automatically: basic, aggregated analytics (pages viewed, where visitors came from). If we run ads, a Meta pixel may record page views and purchases so we can measure them.

What we do with it

Fulfil orders, send the emails you signed up for, answer your questions, and measure which of our posts bring people here. We don't sell your data. We don't share it except with the services that make the site work: Stripe (payments), Klaviyo (email), our shipping carrier, GitHub (hosting), and analytics providers.

Your choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link. To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email hello@themorningupgrades.com and we'll do it within 30 days.

Cookies

The site itself sets none. Stripe and Klaviyo set the ones they need to work. If a Meta pixel is active, it sets its own; you can opt out at facebook.com/ads/preferences.
