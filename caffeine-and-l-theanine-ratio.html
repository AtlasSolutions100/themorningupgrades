Caffeine and L-theanine ratio: what the studies used

By Koby Stull · Updated October 29, 2026

In the studies summarized by a 2021 systematic review, the combinations were 160 mg of caffeine with 200 mg of L-theanine and 150 mg of caffeine with 250 mg of L-theanine: about 1.25 to 1.7 parts L-theanine per part caffeine. The studies were small: these are what was tested, not a recommended dose.

The doses in the review

A 2021 systematic review in Cureus (Sohail et al.) summarized 5 studies of caffeine and L-theanine. The ones that gave both as measured single doses used:

StudyCaffeineL-theaninePeople
Kahathuduwa et al., 2017160 mg200 mg20 healthy adult men
Kahathuduwa et al., 2018160 mg200 mg9 healthy adult men
Haskell et al., 2008150 mg250 mgsmall group of adults

The review notes the 200 mg L-theanine dose in the 2017 study was about what you'd get from 8 cups of black tea. The other 2 studies in the review looked at children with ADHD (5 boys) and at daily matcha capsules, so they don't give a simple coffee-style ratio.

What the ratio means on a label

To find a product's ratio, divide the L-theanine milligrams by the caffeine milligrams on the Supplement Facts panel. A product with 100 mg of L-theanine and 100 mg of caffeine is 1:1; one with 200 mg and 100 mg is 2:1. If ingredients sit inside a proprietary blend, the label may show only the blend's total, and you can't work out the ratio.

What the studies don't tell you

  • They were tiny, and several included only men.
  • They measured short-term lab tasks (reaction time, attention tests), not everyday life.
  • They don't establish a "best" ratio. Nobody compared many ratios head to head.

So the honest answer is: these are the amounts researchers have tested, and that's all they are. This page is not dosing advice; the label's serving is the amount to use.

Counting caffeine

L-theanine doesn't reduce caffeine. For most healthy adults the FDA cites 400 mg of caffeine a day as an amount not generally associated with negative effects, and sensitivity varies. Ask a physician first if you're pregnant, nursing, take medication or have a medical condition.

Questions

What is the best caffeine to L-theanine ratio?

No study has established one. The combinations in a 2021 review were 160 mg caffeine with 200 mg L-theanine, and 150 mg caffeine with 250 mg L-theanine.

Is 1:2 caffeine to L-theanine common?

It's a ratio you'll see on product labels. The studies summarized in the 2021 review used about 1:1.25 to 1:1.7.

How do I work out the ratio of a product?

Divide the L-theanine milligrams by the caffeine milligrams on the label. A proprietary blend may hide the individual amounts.

Does L-theanine cancel out caffeine?

No. It doesn't lower the amount of caffeine in a serving; count it like any other coffee.
