Mushroom coffee vs. MCT coffee: what’s the difference?

By Koby Stull · Updated November 3, 2026

Mushroom coffee is coffee blended with powdered mushroom extracts, most often lion’s mane, reishi, chaga or cordyceps. MCT coffee is coffee with MCT oil or powder added, a fat made from coconut or palm kernel oil. Mushrooms mostly change the flavor and the label. MCT mostly changes the texture: a smoother, creamier cup.

What mushroom coffee is

Mushroom coffee is ground or instant coffee mixed with a powdered mushroom extract. The mushrooms you’ll see most are lion’s mane (Hericium erinaceus), reishi (Ganoderma lucidum), chaga and cordyceps. Labels may say the extract comes from the fruiting body (the mushroom itself) or the mycelium (the root-like part), and may list each mushroom separately or inside a blend.

Some blends swap part of the coffee for mushroom powder, so a serving can hold less coffee, and less caffeine, than your usual cup. Others add caffeine. The label is the only way to know.

What MCT coffee is

MCT stands for medium-chain triglycerides: fats whose chains are 6 to 12 carbons long, usually made from coconut or palm kernel oil. MCT oil is blended into brewed coffee; MCT powder is the oil dried onto a carrier so it stirs in with a spoon. Either way, the cup comes out smoother and creamier without milk. More on MCT coffee.

What’s known about the mushrooms

Human research on these mushrooms is limited. Memorial Sloan Kettering’s About Herbs page on lion’s mane calls the clinical data limited, lists abdominal discomfort, nausea and skin rash as reported side effects, and notes it can interact with some medicines. The National Cancer Institute’s summary on medicinal mushrooms points out that the FDA doesn’t regularly review how supplements are made, so batches and brands may not be the same.

That doesn’t make mushroom coffee a bad choice. It makes the label the place to look. If you take medication or have a medical condition, ask your physician first.

Questions

Does mushroom coffee taste like mushrooms?

It depends on the blend. Coffee is usually the main flavor, and some people notice an earthier note. Blends with less coffee in them taste milder.

Does mushroom coffee have less caffeine?

Only if a serving holds less coffee. Some blends swap part of the coffee for mushroom powder; others add caffeine. Check the label.

Are the mushrooms in mushroom coffee psychedelic?

No. Lion’s mane, reishi, chaga and cordyceps are not psilocybin mushrooms.

Can I add MCT powder to mushroom coffee?

Yes. MCT powder stirs into hot coffee. Read both labels for the serving size and any added caffeine.

Is MCT coffee the same as butter coffee?

No. Butter coffee is brewed coffee blended with butter or ghee, usually plus MCT oil. MCT coffee has MCT oil or powder and no butter.
