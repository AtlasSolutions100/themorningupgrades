Cinnamon instant coffee

By Koby Stull · October 29, 2026

Stir a quarter teaspoon of ground cinnamon into the hot water first, then add one serving of instant coffee and the rest of the water; let it sit a minute and the cinnamon settles instead of floating as grit. Or steep a cinnamon stick in the hot water for two minutes and skip the powder entirely.

  • Prep 3 min
  • Total 4 min
  • Makes 1 mug (about 8 fl oz)

Ingredients

  • 1 serving instant coffee (one level scoop of ours, or the serving size on your jar)
  • 8 fl oz hot water
  • 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon, or 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 tsp brown sugar or honey (optional)
  • Milk or creamer (optional)

Steps

  1. Put the ground cinnamon and the sweetener in the mug and add a splash of the hot water. Stir into a paste.
  2. Add the instant coffee and the rest of the hot water, and stir until dissolved.
  3. Let it sit for a minute so any undissolved cinnamon settles to the bottom.
  4. Add milk or creamer if you like. Leave the last sip in the mug; that's where the sediment is.

Tips

  • Ground cinnamon doesn't dissolve in water, so some sediment is normal. A cinnamon stick avoids it.
  • Mixing cinnamon with the sweetener first helps it disperse.
  • For iced: make the cinnamon base hot, then pour over ice and add cold milk.
  • Ceylon cinnamon tastes softer and sweeter; the common cassia kind is spicier. Either works here.

Questions

Why doesn't cinnamon dissolve in coffee?

Ground cinnamon is bark, not a soluble powder. It disperses but never dissolves, so it settles or floats.

Can I use cinnamon syrup?

Yes. Use about 2 teaspoons and skip the sugar.
